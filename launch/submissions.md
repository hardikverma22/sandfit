## Newsletters

Check each site's "submit" or contact page. Keep the pitch to five lines:

> **Subject:** sandfit: turn on your coding agent's sandbox without breaking your workflow
>
> Hi! I built sandfit, an open-source CLI that reads Claude Code / Codex history and writes a
> least-privilege sandbox policy, and shows what would break before you switch it on. It's tested
> against a real sandbox in CI (26/26 probes; the forecast matched 36/36).
> `npx sandfit` · https://github.com/hardikverma22/sandfit
> Thanks for considering it!

| Newsletter | Fit |
|---|---|
| Console.dev | Best fit: weekly picks of developer tools |
| Changelog News | Open-source projects with a story |
